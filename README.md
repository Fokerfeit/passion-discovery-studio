## Vision

Every child masters the full Québec Education Program curriculum — same destination, personalized path, fuelled by genuine passion.

---

## The Problem

Traditional elementary education applies a single path to all students. In high-need neighbourhoods like Montréal-Nord, this causes:

- Early disengagement (ages 5–7)
- Higher secondary dropout rates
- Greater exposure to risk factors
- Wasted human potential — at scale

High-energy children get labelled as behaviour problems. Cerebral children get bored and switch off. Nobody asks what actually excites them.

---

## The Solution

### Core Components

#### 1. Passion Discovery Studio
- Summer sessions: 7 hands-on stations, 15–20 min per child (1–2 at a time)
- Stations: Metal & tools | Jewelry & diamonds | Microscope | Water physics | Construction (Lego/gears) | Nature | Art & creation
- Open year-round during school breaks, volunteer-run

#### 2. Personalized Learning Paths
- 7–8 simultaneous learning paths per classroom
- Each path maps to the same QEP curriculum endpoints
- High-energy students → more physical activity & outdoor breaks
- Cerebral students → library, Studio, peer discussion, early homework

#### 3. Tablet Program
- Basic locked-down educational tablet (ultra-Orthodox model: focused functions only)
- One device per student, kept for all 5 primary years
- Visual, game-based curriculum delivery (Lumosity-style engagement design)
- No social media. No open internet. No distractions.

#### 4. Evolved Teacher Role
- 60% coaching, differentiated support, IT facilitation
- 40% direct instruction
- Teachers supervise outdoor breaks; volunteers staff the Studio

#### 5. AI-Driven Continuous Improvement
- Simulations using Ministère de l'Éducation du Québec data
- Pre-launch scenario modelling and path optimization
- Evidence-based iterative refinement throughout the pilot

## Why This Exists — Personal Context

I am Luca Urbani. I never connected with school as a child. I became a mechanic out of necessity when my son was born unexpectedly. I spent years as an RCAF Aviation Technician — disciplined, effective, award-winning. But it was not my passion.

At 34, I discovered metal, diamonds, and micrology. Learning became effortless.

I built this project so no child in Montréal-Nord has to wait 34 years to find out what drives them.

My background in aviation diagnostics and high-stakes performance optimization directly informs the design philosophy: identify the problem precisely, test rigorously, optimize continuously, scale what works.
