The Problem

One path doesn't fit every child.

Traditional elementary education applies a single approach to all students. In high-need neighbourhoods like Montréal-Nord, this creates a crisis of disengagement before children even reach secondary school.

The Solution

Five integrated components.
One coherent system.

Every component maps to the same QEP curriculum endpoints — students reach the same destination through paths built around who they actually are.

🔬

1. Passion Discovery Studio

7 hands-on stations (metal & tools, jewelry, microscope, water physics, construction, nature, art). Summer sessions with 15–20 min per child. Open year-round, volunteer-run.

🗺️

2. Personalized Learning Paths

7–8 simultaneous paths per classroom. High-energy students get more movement and outdoor time. Cerebral students get library, Studio, and peer discussion. Same curriculum endpoints for all.

📱

3. Tablet Program

Locked-down educational tablets — one per student for all 5 primary years. No social media, no open internet. Game-based, visual curriculum delivery inspired by proven engagement models.

👩‍🏫

4. Evolved Teacher Role

60% adaptive coaching and differentiated support. 40% direct instruction. Teachers supervise outdoor breaks while trained volunteers staff the Studio.

🤖

5. AI-Driven Optimization

Simulations using Ministère de l'Éducation du Québec data. Pre-launch scenario modelling and path optimization. Evidence-based refinement throughout the pilot.

Why This Exists

A personal mission, not a theory.

I am Luca Urbani. I never connected with school as a child. I became a mechanic out of necessity when my son was born unexpectedly. I spent years as an RCAF Aviation Technician — disciplined, effective, award-winning. But it was not my passion.

At 34, I discovered metal, diamonds, and micrology. Learning became effortless.

I built this project so no child in Montréal-Nord has to wait 34 years to find out what drives them. My background in aviation diagnostics and high-stakes performance optimization directly informs the design philosophy: identify the problem precisely, test rigorously, optimize continuously, scale what works.

"Every child deserves the chance I never had: to discover what truly drives them, and let that fuel their entire education."
